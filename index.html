Our Heritage

Since 1985, we have dedicated ourselves to the pursuit of excellence in wool craftsmanship. Our journey began in the rolling hills of Tasmania, where we established direct partnerships with multi-generational sheep farmers committed to sustainable practices.

Today, our ateliers in Italy combine traditional artisan techniques with modern innovation, creating collections that embody the perfect balance of heritage and contemporary elegance. Each piece is a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality and sustainability.
